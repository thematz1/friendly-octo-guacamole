The house

Built on provenance, discretion and a careful eye for exceptional pieces.

TMG Brokerage was founded on a simple conviction: the world's finest pieces deserve a private stage, not a public auction.

We operate as a closed brokerage, matching verified collectors with authenticated pieces through personal consultation. Our clients don't browse. We present them with opportunities selected for them.

Every piece we handle carries a complete provenance record, and every transaction stays confidential.

What we hold to

Provenance first
We reject what we cannot verify. Our multi-point authentication traces every piece back to its origin, with no exceptions.
Discretion
Our clients' identities and collections stay private. No public listings and no open bidding, only confidential introductions arranged by a broker.
Selective membership
Access is earned, not purchased. Every client has a personal review, so members share the same standards.
